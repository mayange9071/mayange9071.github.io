📖 Step-by-Step: Use It in 5 Minutes (Windows)

Step 1: Open X/Twitter in Google Chrome or Edge

Open your browser (Chrome or Edge works best). Go to www.x.com and log in. Make sure you see your profile timeline loaded normally.

Step 2: Open the Page Source Console

Press F12 on your keyboard. This opens the developer tools. On the top menu bar of that panel, click the tab that says "Console".

Step 3: Copy the Script Text

Open the delete-all-tweets.js file you downloaded using Notepad (right-click the file → Open with → Notepad). Inside, you'll see lots of code. Select it all (Ctrl+A) and copy it (Ctrl+C).

Step 4: Paste and Run

Click inside the Console panel once. Then paste with Ctrl+V. Finally, press Enter.

⚠️ The script will start immediately. You'll see a message saying "Starting deletion of 897 tweets..." Do not close the tab — let it work.

Step 5: Watch It Work & Wait for the Summary

The script will slowly scroll through your profile, deleting/undoing actions. You'll notice your counts going down. When it finishes, it lists a summary like:

🛠️ Troubleshooting (Simple Fixes)

Issue: "Script is not running" / nothing happens

Fix: Make sure you pressed Enter at the end. Also confirm you are logged into X and that you are on x.com, not twitter.com (redirect happens automatically). Refresh the page and try again.

Issue: It stops mid-way

Fix: Sometimes X sends a "rate limit" or refresh prompt. The script retries automatically. Just leave the tab open after pressing Enter — don't touch it. If it's stuck past 10 minutes, reload the page (F5), log in if prompted, and re-paste the script.

Issue: "Unexpected token" error in console

Fix: You didn't copy the entire script. Re-open the file in Notepad, press Ctrl+A to select everything, then Ctrl+C to copy. Then in console, click the small broom icon (🖱️ "Clear console") and paste again.
